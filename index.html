Popsicles: Sweet & Refreshing All Year Long!

Fruit Popsicles

Ingredients

  • 2 cups fresh blueberries, raspberries, strawberries, and sliced bananas, mixed
  • 2 cups plain or vanilla yogurt
  • 1/4 cup white sugar or to taste
  • 8 small paper cups or popsicle molds
  • 8 popsicle sticks

Steps

  1. Gather all ingredients
  2. Place the mixed fruit, yogurt, and sugar into a blender. Blend until chunky or smooth.
  3. Fill paper cups or popsicle molds 3/4 full with fruit mixture.
  4. Cover with aluminum foil and poke a popsicle stick through the center.
  5. Freeze for at least 5 hours. Remove foil and cups/molds to serve.
  6. Enjoy!

Nutritional Facts

(per serving)

  • Calories: 83
  • Fat: 1g
  • Carbs: 16g
  • Protein: 4g

Double Chocolate Fudge Pops

Ingredients

  • ½ cup white sugar
  • 2 tablespoons cornstarch
  • 2 tablespoons cocoa powder
  • 2 ½ cups whole milk
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
  • 1 tablespoon butter
  • ¼ cup miniature semisweet chocolate chips
  • popsicle molds

Steps

  1. Gather all ingredients.
  2. Combine sugar, cornstarch, cocoa powder, and milk in a small saucepan over medium heat. Bring to a simmer and stir until thick, about 2 minutes. Remove from heat; stir in vanilla and butter.
  3. Transfer to a heatproof bowl and refrigerate until cool, approximately 20 minutes.
  4. Stir chocolate chips into the cooled chocolate mixture.
  5. Pour into your favorite popsicle molds and freeze until solid, about 4 hours.
  6. To serve, run warm water over the molds until fudge pops slide out easily.
  7. Enjoy!

Nutritional Facts

(per serving)

  • Calories: 145
  • Fat: 6g
  • Carbs: 22g
  • Protein: 3g

Acai Bowl

Ingredients

  • 7 ounces frozen unsweetened acai pulp, partially defrosted in refrigerator overnight
  • 2 medium ripe frozen bananas
  • 1 cup blueberries
  • ¾ cup almond milk, or more as needed
  • 2 teaspoons honey, or to taste

Toppings

  • 1 medium banana, sliced
  • 2 tablespoons fresh blueberries, or to taste
  • 1 tablespoon pomegranate seeds, or to taste
  • 1 tablespoon large coconut flakes, or to taste

Steps

  1. Combine acai, bananas, blueberries, almond milk, and honey in a blender. Blend until smooth. Smoothie should have a thick consistency, like frozen yogurt.
  2. Pour into 2 bowls and top with banana slices, blueberries, pomegranate seeds, and coconut.

Cooking Notes

  • I take the acai pulp out of the freezer and let it defrost a bit overnight in the fridge for easier blending.

Nutrition Facts

  • Calories: 347
  • Fat: 9g
  • Carbs: 68g
  • Protein: 3g
